§1 · Vector Algebra

Products PYQ★★

Dot: \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\cos\theta\).   Cross: \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|=|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta\), direction by RHR.

Scalar triple product: \([\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b},\mathbf{c}]=\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})=\begin{vmatrix}a_1&a_2&a_3\\b_1&b_2&b_3\\c_1&c_2&c_3\end{vmatrix}\)

= volume of parallelepiped. Cyclic: \([\mathbf{a}\,\mathbf{b}\,\mathbf{c}]=[\mathbf{b}\,\mathbf{c}\,\mathbf{a}]=[\mathbf{c}\,\mathbf{a}\,\mathbf{b}]\).

Vector triple product: \(\mathbf{a}\times(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})=(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c})\mathbf{b}-(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b})\mathbf{c}\)  ("BAC-CAB rule").

§2 · Gradient, Divergence, Curl

Del Operator \(\nabla\)

\(\nabla = \mathbf{i}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial x}+\mathbf{j}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial y}+\mathbf{k}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial z}\)

Gradient PYQ★★★

\(\text{grad}\,\phi = \nabla\phi = \left(\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial x},\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial y},\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial z}\right)\)

  • Points in direction of max rate of increase of \(\phi\).
  • Magnitude \(|\nabla\phi|\) = max rate of change.
  • Directional derivative: \(D_{\hat u}\phi = \nabla\phi\cdot\hat u\).
  • \(\nabla\phi\) is normal to the level surface \(\phi=\text{const}\).
  • Unit normal to surface \(F=0\): \(\hat n=\dfrac{\nabla F}{|\nabla F|}\).
Divergence PYQ★★★

\(\text{div}\,\mathbf{F}=\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}=\dfrac{\partial F_1}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial F_2}{\partial y}+\dfrac{\partial F_3}{\partial z}\)

Physical meaning: net outward flux per unit volume. Source if \(>0\), sink if \(<0\).

\(\mathbf{F}\) is solenoidal iff \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}=0\).

Curl PYQ★★★

\(\text{curl}\,\mathbf{F}=\nabla\times\mathbf{F}=\begin{vmatrix}\mathbf{i}&\mathbf{j}&\mathbf{k}\\\partial_x&\partial_y&\partial_z\\F_1&F_2&F_3\end{vmatrix}\)

Physical meaning: rotation/circulation per unit area.

\(\mathbf{F}\) is irrotational iff \(\nabla\times\mathbf{F}=\mathbf{0}\).

Laplacian

\(\nabla^2\phi = \nabla\cdot(\nabla\phi) = \dfrac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial x^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial y^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial z^2}\)

\(\phi\) is harmonic iff \(\nabla^2\phi=0\).

§3 · Vector Identities

Key Identities PYQ★★★
  • \(\nabla(\phi\psi)=\phi\nabla\psi+\psi\nabla\phi\)
  • \(\nabla\cdot(\phi\mathbf{F})=\phi\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}+\mathbf{F}\cdot\nabla\phi\)
  • \(\nabla\times(\phi\mathbf{F})=\phi(\nabla\times\mathbf{F})+(\nabla\phi)\times\mathbf{F}\)
  • \(\nabla\cdot(\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B})=\mathbf{B}\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{A})-\mathbf{A}\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{B})\)
  • \(\nabla\times(\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B})=\mathbf{A}(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B})-\mathbf{B}(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{A})+(\mathbf{B}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{A}-(\mathbf{A}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{B}\)
  • \(\nabla\times(\nabla\phi)=\mathbf{0}\)  (curl of gradient is zero)
  • \(\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{F})=0\)  (div of curl is zero)
  • \(\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf{F})=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F})-\nabla^2\mathbf{F}\)
  • \(\nabla(r^n)=nr^{n-2}\mathbf{r}\).   \(\nabla^2(r^n)=n(n+1)r^{n-2}\).   \(\nabla^2(1/r)=0\) for \(r\ne 0\).
  • \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{r}=3\).   \(\nabla\times\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{0}\).   \(\nabla r=\hat r=\mathbf{r}/r\).

§4 · Line Integrals

Line Integral PYQ★★★

\(\displaystyle\int_C\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}=\int_C(F_1\,dx+F_2\,dy+F_3\,dz)\)

Parametrize \(C\): \(\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{r}(t)\), then \(d\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{r}'(t)\,dt\).

Work done by force \(\mathbf{F}\) along \(C\) = \(\int_C\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}\).

§5 · Surface Integrals

Flux Integral PYQ★★★

\(\displaystyle\iint_S\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{S}=\iint_S\mathbf{F}\cdot\hat n\,dS\)

For surface \(z=g(x,y)\): \(d\mathbf{S}=(-g_x,-g_y,1)\,dx\,dy\) (upward normal).

For surface \(F(x,y,z)=0\): \(\hat n=\dfrac{\nabla F}{|\nabla F|}\), \(dS=\dfrac{dA}{|\hat n\cdot\hat k|}\) where \(dA\) is projected area element.

§6 · Green's Theorem

Green's Theorem in the Plane PYQ★★★

\[\oint_C(M\,dx+N\,dy)=\iint_D\left(\frac{\partial N}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial M}{\partial y}\right)dA\]

\(C\) = positively oriented (counterclockwise) boundary of \(D\).

Area formula: \(A=\dfrac{1}{2}\oint_C(x\,dy-y\,dx)\).

Green's first identity: \(\iint_D\phi\nabla^2\psi\,dA=\oint_C\phi\dfrac{\partial\psi}{\partial n}\,ds-\iint_D\nabla\phi\cdot\nabla\psi\,dA\)

§7 · Stokes' Theorem

Stokes' Theorem PYQ★★★

\[\oint_C\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}=\iint_S(\nabla\times\mathbf{F})\cdot d\mathbf{S}\]

\(C\) = positively oriented boundary of surface \(S\) (right-hand rule: curl fingers along \(C\), thumb points in \(\hat n\) direction).

Consequence: For irrotational \(\mathbf{F}\) (\(\nabla\times\mathbf{F}=0\)): line integral around any closed curve = 0, integral is path-independent.

§8 · Gauss's Divergence Theorem

Gauss Divergence Theorem PYQ★★★

\[\oiint_S\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{S}=\iiint_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}\,dV\]

\(S\) = closed surface bounding volume \(V\), outward normal.

Applications: \(\oiint_S\hat n\,dS=\mathbf{0}\).   \(\oiint_S\mathbf{r}\cdot d\mathbf{S}=3V\).   \(\oiint_S\mathbf{F}\times d\mathbf{S}=-\iiint\nabla\times\mathbf{F}\,dV\).

Green's theorem in 3D (1st identity): \(\iiint_V\phi\nabla^2\psi\,dV=\oiint_S\phi\dfrac{\partial\psi}{\partial n}\,dS-\iiint_V\nabla\phi\cdot\nabla\psi\,dV\).

Green's 2nd identity: \(\iiint_V(\phi\nabla^2\psi-\psi\nabla^2\phi)\,dV=\oiint_S\left(\phi\dfrac{\partial\psi}{\partial n}-\psi\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial n}\right)dS\).

§9 · Conservative Fields & Scalar Potential

Conservative Field — Equivalences PYQ★★★

The following are equivalent for a smooth \(\mathbf{F}\) on a simply connected domain:

  • \(\mathbf{F}=\nabla\phi\) for some scalar \(\phi\) (potential).
  • \(\nabla\times\mathbf{F}=\mathbf{0}\) (irrotational).
  • \(\oint_C\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}=0\) for every closed curve \(C\).
  • \(\int_A^B\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}\) is path-independent.
Finding Scalar Potential

If \(\mathbf{F}=\nabla\phi\), then \(\phi_x=F_1,\;\phi_y=F_2,\;\phi_z=F_3\). Integrate \(\phi_x\) w.r.t. \(x\), use \(\phi_y\) to find \(g(y,z)\), then \(\phi_z\) for the rest.

§10 · Curvilinear Coordinates

Cylindrical Coordinates \((r,\theta,z)\) PYQ★★

\(x=r\cos\theta,\;y=r\sin\theta,\;z=z\). Scale factors: \(h_r=1,\;h_\theta=r,\;h_z=1\).

\(\nabla\phi=\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial r}\hat r+\dfrac{1}{r}\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial\theta}\hat\theta+\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial z}\hat z\)

\(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{F}=\dfrac{1}{r}\dfrac{\partial(rF_r)}{\partial r}+\dfrac{1}{r}\dfrac{\partial F_\theta}{\partial\theta}+\dfrac{\partial F_z}{\partial z}\)

\(\nabla^2\phi=\dfrac{1}{r}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial r}\!\left(r\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial r}\right)+\dfrac{1}{r^2}\dfrac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial\theta^2}+\dfrac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial z^2}\)

Spherical Coordinates \((\rho,\phi,\theta)\) PYQ★★★

\(x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\;y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\;z=\rho\cos\phi\). Scale factors: \(h_\rho=1,\;h_\phi=\rho,\;h_\theta=\rho\sin\phi\).

\(\nabla^2\phi=\dfrac{1}{\rho^2}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial\rho}\!\left(\rho^2\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial\rho}\right)+\dfrac{1}{\rho^2\sin\phi}\dfrac{\partial}{\partial\phi}\!\left(\sin\phi\dfrac{\partial\phi}{\partial\phi}\right)+\dfrac{1}{\rho^2\sin^2\phi}\dfrac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial\theta^2}\)

Volume element: \(dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta\).

Quick Application Guide
  • Flux out of closed surface → Gauss's theorem → volume integral.
  • Line integral around closed loop → Stokes' → surface integral.
  • Plane closed loop → Green's theorem.
  • Check \(\nabla\times\mathbf{F}=0\) before claiming conservative.
  • Simply connected domain crucial — \(\mathbf{F}=\dfrac{-y\,\hat i+x\,\hat j}{x^2+y^2}\) has curl 0 but is not conservative on \(\mathbb{R}^2\setminus\{0\}\).
